## [0.4.0](https://github.com/claytercek/godot-hap-video/compare/v0.3.0...v0.4.0) (2026-10-21)

### Added
* Play embedded 16-bit and 24-bit integer PCM audio from the first audio track
  in Hap MOV files through HapPlayer and VideoStreamPlayer. Mono and stereo,
  little- and big-endian samples, and sample rates from 8,000 to 192,000 Hz are
  supported. Unsupported audio warns without preventing video playback.
  HapPlayer audio is muted outside forward playback at 1x speed; synchronization
  is approximate, not sample-exact or gapless.
